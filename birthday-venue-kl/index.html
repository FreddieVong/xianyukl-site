Birthday and group nights in KL

Planning a birthday or a night out with friends? Book a table at Xianyu 咸鱼酒馆 in Taman Desa. There is live music every night, the kitchen is open till 1am, and the band sings for the birthday person.

Book a birthday table on WhatsApp Every night · 9:30pmLive band every nightThis week ›

Birthday perks

  • A free mini cake or Flaming Lamborghini, 3 days either side of your birthday. Claim it in Xianyu Play (free to join, nothing to download).
  • The band sings for you.
  • Live music every night, 9:30pm to 12:45am

Booking a group

  • WhatsApp +60 12-652 2885 with date, time and pax. No deposit.
  • Sofas: RM500 minimum spend for up to 7 guests, or RM1,000 for the big sofa (up to 12). Only on Fri, Sat and public-holiday eves; no minimum on weekdays.
  • To share: fruit towers RM128 to RM228, beer buckets (5 bottles) RM118 to RM138, Moët 6 bottles RM2,800
  • Bottle keeping: spirits 60 days, beer 30 days
  • Strictly 18+

Prices before 10% service charge and 8% SST.

Questions guests ask

What does Xianyu give me on my birthday?

Join Xianyu Play for free. 3 days either side of your birthday, claim a free mini cake or Flaming Lamborghini in the app. The band sings for you.

How many people fit on a sofa?

A sofa takes up to 7 guests and the big sofa up to 12.

Is there a minimum spend?

Sofas on Fri, Sat and public-holiday eves only: RM500 (up to 7 guests) or RM1,000 big sofa (up to 12). No minimum on weekdays.

How do I book?

WhatsApp +60 12-652 2885 with date, time and pax. No deposit; tables are held about 15 minutes. Walk-ins welcome.

What time is live music at Xianyu?

Live music every night from 9:30pm to 12:45am.

Can under-18s come?

No. Xianyu is strictly 18+ at all hours.

Where is Xianyu?

Unit A-G-03, Union Tower, Jalan Desa Bakti, Taman Desa, 58000 Kuala Lumpur, off Old Klang Road, south of Mid Valley. Search "Xian Yu KL" on Waze or Grab.
