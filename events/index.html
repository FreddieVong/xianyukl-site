Questions guests ask

Can we book all of Xianyu for an event?

Yes, from Sunday to Thursday, but not on a public-holiday eve. Up to 180 guests. The minimum spend is RM15,000, before 10% service charge and SST.

We are 50 people or fewer. Do we need the whole venue?

No. Book part of the venue and order from the menu as usual. There is no extra charge.

Can a bigger group book part of the venue?

Yes. WhatsApp us to check space. Only whole-venue events get the stage.

What counts toward the minimum spend?

The buffet, at RM50–RM60 per person, and anything from the menu. Promotions don’t count.

Do you have free-flow drinks?

No. Drinks are ordered from the menu, and they count toward the minimum spend.

What comes with the whole venue?

A 3-piece band playing 3 sets of 45 minutes. The stage and a mic for speeches and lucky draws. Your guests can sing with the band. Bring your own decorations, or ask us to arrange a decorator.

What time can the event start and end?

We agree the times with you when you book.

How do we pay?

A 50% deposit (RM7,500) holds your date. You pay the rest, plus 10% service charge and SST, on the night.

Can we change the date?

Yes, once. Your deposit moves to the new date. It is not refunded in cash.
