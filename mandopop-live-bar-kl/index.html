Questions guests ask

What time is live music at Xianyu?

Live music every night from 9:30pm to 12:45am.

What are the opening hours?

Daily 6:30pm to 2:30am. The kitchen closes at 1am.

Where is Xianyu?

Unit A-G-03, Union Tower, Jalan Desa Bakti, Taman Desa, 58000 Kuala Lumpur. Search "Xian Yu KL" on Waze or Grab.

How do I book?

WhatsApp +60 12-652 2885 with date, time and pax. No deposit; tables are held about 15 minutes. Walk-ins welcome.

Is there a happy hour?

Before 8:30pm, Sunday to Thursday (not public-holiday eves): beer bucket buy 1 free 1, Guinness 10 glasses RM175.

Can kids come?

No. Xianyu is strictly 18+ at all hours.

Is there a minimum spend?

Sofas on Fri, Sat and public-holiday eves only: RM500 (up to 7 guests) or RM1,000 big sofa (up to 12). No minimum on weekdays.

Can I smoke?

No cigarettes indoors. Vapes are OK.
