Company dinners and team nights in Taman Desa

Planning a company dinner, a team night or a year-end makan? Xianyu 咸鱼酒馆 has sofas for groups, a live Mandopop band every night and a kitchen open till 1am.

WhatsApp us your date, time and number of people and we will hold the right table. No deposit.

Weekday company groups

  • Sunday to Thursday there is no minimum spend on sofas, except on a public-holiday eve
  • Mondays are the easiest night for a company group
  • Sofas for up to 7, or the big sofa for up to 12
  • Before 8:30pm, Sunday to Thursday: beer bucket buy 1 free 1 (not on public-holiday eves)
  • Live band 9:30pm to 12:45am every night, kitchen till 1am
  • Strictly 18+

How to book

  • WhatsApp +60 12-652 2885 with your date, time and number of people
  • No deposit; tables are held about 15 minutes
  • Walk-ins are welcome daily

Friday, Saturday and public-holiday eves

  • Minimum spend on sofas: RM500 (up to 7) or RM1,000 (big sofa, up to 12)
  • Spirits can be kept for 60 days, beer for 30 days

Questions guests ask

How many people can Xianyu seat in one group?

A sofa fits up to 7, and the big sofa fits up to 12. WhatsApp us for bigger groups.

Is there a minimum spend on a weekday?

Sunday to Thursday there is no minimum spend on sofas, except on a public-holiday eve. On Friday, Saturday and public-holiday eves the sofa minimum is RM500 (up to 7) or RM1,000 (big sofa, up to 12).

Do I need to pay a deposit?

No deposit. Tables are held about 15 minutes.

Is there a set menu for groups?

Guests order from the food and drinks menu. WhatsApp us with your date and number of people and we will hold the right table.

Where is Xianyu?

Unit A-G-03, Union Tower, Jalan Desa Bakti, Taman Desa, 58000 Kuala Lumpur. Search "Xian Yu KL" on Waze or Grab.

Can under-18s come?

No. Xianyu is strictly 18+ at all hours.
